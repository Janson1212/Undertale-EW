# Undertale-EW
An Undertale fangame that is currently working in process.
This README is here to give you a quick rundown of what we are building.

Instead of a normal trio fight, where each sans changes their phases according to the trio phases, each sans will change phases after meeting a certain condition, making each of them has a bone-afide mind of their own. In order to add more depth into the game, we also added different endings to each sans if player "ACT" differently.

The Bigger Picture
We wanna make a whole game instead of just the trio fight, we are just starting off with making the trio fight to establish the core mechanics first, as it is the most humerus and engaging part of our vision.

We are aiming to release our first beta before October.
